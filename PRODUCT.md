# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Locals, expats, and tourists in Da Nang, Vietnam researching dental care. Expats and tourists are often anxious about finding a trustworthy English-speaking dentist abroad; locals compare clinics on reputation and price. All arrive via Google Maps or search and spend under two minutes deciding whether to book. Many are on mobile, outdoors, or on low-bandwidth connections.

## Product Purpose

Convert website visitors into booked patients. The site's sole job is to earn trust fast and lower the friction to contacting Dr. Hoa. Success = a call, WhatsApp message, or completed booking form.

## Brand Personality

Warm · Trustworthy · Modern. The clinic is led by a specific person (Dr. Hoa), bilingual, and welcoming to foreigners — this should read as a real practice with expertise and genuine care, not a faceless chain.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / tech startup: purple-blue gradients, Inter everywhere, hero-metric cards, glassmorphism, rounded-icon grid tiles above every heading.
- Cookie-cutter Wix/Squarespace template: looks identical to every other local business site, zero visual differentiation, stock-photo smiles, layout that screams "free website builder."

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the feature.** Every design decision should ask: does this make a nervous first-time patient feel safe? Credentials, real photos, and specific social proof outrank decoration.
2. **Person over brand.** Dr. Hoa is a name and a face, not a logo. Humanise; avoid the cold "our team" abstraction.
3. **Speed of clarity.** A patient scanning in 30 seconds should know: what services exist, rough prices, how to contact, and where the clinic is. Don't bury this under scroll-bait.
4. **Mobile-first, genuinely.** The majority of the audience is on phone in a browser. CTAs, contact links, and the booking form must work flawlessly at 375px.
5. **Warmth without kitsch.** Modern and elevated, but not sterile or tech-cold. Warmth through typography, photo choice, and spacing — not through clipart teeth or teal-and-white clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Both English and Vietnamese content must be equally legible at full contrast. Reduced-motion preference respected for any animations added. Floating contact buttons require visible focus states and ARIA labels.
